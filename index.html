⭐
The star: "towing yarra valley"

$23 per lead at a 9.6% CTR — the best keyword in the account. The geographic sweet spot; worth more budget and more town variations around it.

🚚
The volume engine

The two broad "near me / melbourne" terms deliver 10.5 of 28 leads — the pricier end, but they're the volume.

⚠️
The drag on CPC: "tow truck near me"

Spent $149 for a single lead at Quality Score 4 — the worst value of any keyword. Paired with "tow trucks near me" at Quality Score 1, these low-relevance near-me terms are inflating cost across the board. A tighter, more relevant landing page lifts Quality Score and pulls CPC back down.

05 · Wins

The wins 🏆

📈
Conversions up every single month

17 → 25 → 28. That's +65% from April to June, and the strongest month on record.

🎯
Click-through rate up 11×

From 0.58% to 6.41%. We cut broad, low-intent traffic and put budget behind tight search — far fewer impressions, but the right people clicking.

💰
Cost per lead down ~40% vs April

$91 → $54, with May the standout at just $43 per conversion.

⚡
More results from less noise

June drove 28 conversions off just 2,045 impressions. April needed 66,000 impressions for 17. A far more efficient account.

06 · Action plan

Worth watching

⚠️
June CPC jumped to $11.63

Up from $3.20 in May — likely increased competition or a bid-strategy shift. It's the one thing pulling cost-per-lead back up. Reining it in gets us under $40 per lead.

📊
Conversion value isn't tracked yet

Conversions are counted but not valued, so we can't report revenue/ROAS. An average job or lead value unlocks dollar-return reporting.

Recommended next steps

  • Search terms audit — find what's driving the June CPC creep and add negatives to cut waste.
  • Set a conversion value — so next month's report shows actual return, not just lead count.
  • Decide on the paused campaigns — a "Vic Haulage Co New" search campaign and two P-Max campaigns are sitting idle.
