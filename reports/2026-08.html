The headline: we spent 15% less and got 20% fewer leads. Cost per lead rose only slightly, from $43.52 to $45.91 — so the account didn't get less efficient so much as it got smaller. Two things drove that: the daily budget came down partway through the month, and the ads were switched off entirely on seven of the month's thirty-one days.

Underneath the smaller numbers, the campaign actually sharpened. Click-through rate hit 7.73% and conversion rate 34.5% — both the best figures the account has recorded.

⭐
"yarra valley towing" carried the month

Across both campaigns it produced 12.5 of the month's 41 leads at $24.43 each, from 16% of the spend. It is comfortably the most efficient lead source in the account.

🆕
A dedicated Yarra Valley campaign went live on 22 August

It only ran for the last ten days of the month and already returned 2 leads at $32.86 each. Small numbers so far, but the early signal is consistent with everything else the Yarra Valley terms have done.

🎯
Suburb terms keep pulling extraordinary engagement

Healesville 60% CTR, Bayswater 50%, Pakenham 28%, Berwick 22% — against 3.6% for "towing service melbourne". When someone searches their own town, they click. Healesville, Bayswater and Berwick didn't convert this month, but they did it on very small click volumes.

⚠️
"towing service melbourne" is the most expensive habit in the account

It took $501 — a quarter of the entire month's budget — and returned 7 leads at $71.60 each, at a 3.6% click-through rate. Every local term in the table beats it. That budget would go considerably further pointed at towns.

📉
Quality Score on "tow truck near me" slipped to 3

Down from 4 in July, which means Google is charging more per click for the same position. The good news is the rest of the account moved the other way — "tow trucks near me" recovered from 1 to 5 and "towing service melbourne" from 5 to 7.

05 · Wins

The wins 🏆

⚡
Best click-through rate and best conversion rate on record

CTR 7.73% and conversion rate 34.5%, both up on July. More than one in three clicks now becomes an enquiry. The traffic reaching the site is the most qualified it has ever been.

🗺️
The local strategy took over the account

Local town and Yarra Valley terms produced 59% of the leads from 47% of the spend, at $37.13 against $58.30 for broad metro. In July local terms were roughly a third of spend — the mix has moved decisively the right way.

🆕
A Yarra Valley campaign is now live

Launched 22 August and converting from its first week. It gives the strongest part of the account its own budget rather than competing with metro terms for the same pool.

💵
Cost per lead held up on much lower spend

Spend fell 15% and cost per lead rose only $2.39. Efficiency was largely protected through a shrinking month — it is still roughly half what it was in April.

06 · Action plan

Worth watching

🌙
The ads were dark on 7 of the month's 31 days

No impressions at all on Saturday 1st, Sunday 2nd, Saturday 8th, and then every Sunday for the rest of the month (9th, 16th, 23rd, 30th). Towing demand does not stop at the weekend — breakdowns and accidents are arguably more likely on a Sunday. This is the single clearest volume opportunity in the account.

💸
Daily budget appears to have been reduced mid-month

Both campaigns now sit at $50/day; the main campaign was on $80/day through July. Spend per running day fell from $96.49 across 3–15 August to $63.13 across 17–31 August. That single change explains most of the drop in lead volume — the account did not stop working, it was given less to work with. Worth confirming the reduction was intended.

📈
Average cost per click rose again — $14.70 → $15.82

A third consecutive monthly increase. Lead quality is absorbing it for now, but it steadily erodes how much a fixed budget can buy.

🚫
$506.87 produced no conversions at all

That is 27% of the month's spend. Much of it sits on local terms that simply had a quiet month and are worth keeping. But roughly $161 is clearly blockable — competitor names (sheen towing bayswater, road angels towing, anytime towing melbourne), off-service queries (tow truck hire, tilt tray hire, car transport melbourne, interstate towing, emergency motorcycle towing) and towns outside the service area (geelong towing services, towing tullamarine).

📊
Conversion value still isn't tracked

Leads are counted but not valued, so we still cannot report revenue or return on ad spend. An average job value is all that's needed to unlock dollar-return reporting. This has now been outstanding for five months and is the one thing holding the reporting back.

Recommended next steps

  • Confirm the budget position — both campaigns are on $50/day against $80/day in July. If the reduction wasn't deliberate, restoring it is the fastest route back to July's lead volume.
  • Turn the ads on seven days a week — seven dark days cost a full week of a month that only converts when it's running. Weekend towing demand is real.
  • Add the negative keyword list — competitor names, hire and interstate-transport queries, and out-of-area towns. Recovers roughly $161/month immediately.
  • Feed the Yarra Valley campaign — it is the cheapest lead source in the account and has had ten days and a small budget. Give it room and add the neighbouring towns.
  • Rebalance away from "towing service melbourne" — a quarter of the budget at $71.60 a lead, when local terms return leads at $37.13.
  • Set a conversion value — so next month's report can show actual return, not just lead count.
