Performance snapshot

The headline, stated plainly: we spent 15% more and got 7% fewer leads. Cost per lead went from $45.91 to $57.01 — a 24% increase and the dearest month since April. This is the account's weakest month on efficiency since the current structure was put in place.

What did not go wrong is the traffic. Impressions rose 23% to 1,886 and clicks rose 18% to 141, while the average cost per click actually fell from $15.82 to $15.36 — the first decline in four months. We bought more, and we bought it slightly cheaper.

The gap sits entirely in conversion rate, which fell from 34.45% to 26.95%. Two identifiable things account for most of it: the Yarra Valley campaign spent $369.95 in six serving days for 2.5 leads before being paused on 07/09/2026, and the broad "melbourne / near me" half of the account took a larger share of the budget than in August while getting dearer per lead.

⭐
"yarra valley towing" carried the month again

9.67 of the month's 38 leads at $26.53 each, from 12% of the spend and a 25.6% click-through rate. It is the fourth month running that this single term has been the most efficient lead source in the account.

🎯
The cheapest leads were all suburb terms

"towing lilydale" $10.60, "tow truck healesville" $11.16, "tow truck pakenham" $26.30. Every lead that cost under $30 came from a term naming a town. Not one came from a broad metro term.

📈
"towing service melbourne" improved markedly

Still the biggest single line of spend at $516.61, but cost per lead fell from $71.60 to $64.58 and CTR nearly doubled from 3.6% to 6.2%. The ad and keyword work on it is clearly landing. It is still dearer than every local term in the table.

📉
"car towing service near me" went backwards badly

$460.70 for 6 leads — $76.78 each, against $43.50 in August on slightly less spend. It is now the account's worst lead cost at volume. Google rates its landing page experience below average, the only keyword in the account carrying that flag, which both raises its click cost and points at where the fix is.

💸
"yarra valley towing healesville" cost $263.24 for 2 leads

$131.62 a lead — the single worst line in the month, and jarring given that plain "yarra valley towing" delivered at $26.53. Almost all of it ($253.69) was the broad-match version in the Yarra Valley campaign, where it drew a 5.0% CTR against 25.6% for the main campaign's version.

🔢
Three of the four biggest-spending keywords sit at Quality Score 5

"car towing service near me", "yarra valley towing" and "tow truck near me" are all on 5 today; "towing service melbourne" is on 7. By contrast "towing croydon" and "tow truck croydon" sit on 8. Every point of Quality Score is a discount on the click price, so the account's largest costs are also where it is paying the biggest penalty.

05 · Wins

The wins 🏆

🌞
The weekend blackout is fixed

Last month's report flagged seven dark days, including every Sunday. In September the ads ran on 29 of 30 days — the only day with no impressions at all was Sunday 06/09/2026. That was the clearest volume opportunity in the account and it has been closed.

💰
Average cost per click fell for the first time in four months

$15.82 → $15.36. After three consecutive rises this is a genuine break in the trend, and it means the budget is buying more clicks per dollar than it did in August.

📣
Reach grew again after three months of shrinking

Impressions up 23% to 1,886 and clicks up 18% to 141 — the first increase in either since June. The account is no longer contracting.

🗺️
The local strategy keeps proving itself

$45.58 per lead against $71.89 for broad metro terms, and 13.3% CTR against 5.3%. Local terms returned 57% of the leads from 45% of the spend. The case for pushing further in this direction is now four months deep.

06 · Action plan

Worth watching

🚨
Cost per lead rose 24% to $57.01

The honest read: this is the account's weakest efficiency month since April. Conversion rate fell from 34.45% to 26.95%, which is where the entire deterioration sits — click costs went down, not up. Two months ago the account was at $43.52 a lead. Getting back under $50 is the priority for October.

🏔️
The Yarra Valley campaign was expensive and is now paused

It spent $369.95 in six serving days (01/09–05/09 and 07/09) for 2.5 leads — $147.98 each, at a $37.00 average cost per click, roughly 2.4× the account average. It was paused on 07/09/2026. Of its spend, $126.26 went to three broad queries — tow truck online quote, towing cost and bare towing — which have since been excluded. In August, over ten days, the same campaign had returned leads at $32.86. The structure, not the geography, is what failed: it was set loose on broad match with no negative list.

🚫
$673.12 went to search terms that produced no leads

34 terms, 31% of the month's spend, up from $506.87 and 27% in August. Roughly $378.52 is clearly blockable: competitor names ($152.56 — nationwide towing number, nationwide towing dandenong, anytime towing, ace towing, magnet towing, racv roadside assist, planet towing melbourne, mendem towing service), price-shopping queries ($111.35 — tow truck online quote, towing cost, how much is a tow truck cost, car towing cost), work the business doesn't do ($95.28 — interstate vehicle transfer, heavy tow truck, accident allocation towing) and out-of-area towns ($19.34 — sunbury towing). The rest is mostly local terms that simply had a quiet month and are worth keeping.

📍
Broad metro terms took back budget share

54.8% of spend in September against 52.7% in August, at $71.89 a lead. Reversing that two-point drift alone would have saved roughly $25 a lead on the money involved. "towing service melbourne" and "car towing service near me" together consumed 45% of the entire month's budget and returned 14 of 38 leads.

📊
Conversion value still isn't tracked

Leads are counted but not valued, so we still cannot report revenue or return on ad spend. An average job value is all that's needed. This has been outstanding for six months and remains the single thing holding the reporting back — it is also what would settle whether a $57 lead is good or bad, rather than merely dearer than last month.

Recommended next steps

  • Add the negative keyword list this week — competitor names, price-shopping queries, interstate and heavy-haulage work, and out-of-area towns. Recovers roughly $378 a month immediately and nothing in it has ever converted.
  • Decide the Yarra Valley campaign's future before restarting it — either relaunch on exact match only with the negative list applied, or fold the Yarra Valley terms back into the main campaign, where "yarra valley towing" returns leads at $26.53 against $147.98 in the dedicated campaign. Restarting it unchanged would repeat September.
  • Fix the landing page behind "car towing service near me" — Google rates its landing page experience below average, the only such flag in the account, on the keyword that now produces the account's dearest leads at volume. This is the highest-leverage single fix available.
  • Shift budget from metro to towns — broad terms are 58% dearer per lead and took 55% of the budget. Moving even ten points of spend towards suburb terms is worth several leads a month at current rates.
  • Confirm the budget position — the main campaign reads $65.00/day today and the paused Yarra Valley campaign $40.00/day. September averaged $61.94 across its 29 serving days. Worth confirming $65/day is the intended level for October before volume is read against it.
  • Set a conversion value — six months in, this is the only thing standing between this report and genuine return-on-spend reporting.
