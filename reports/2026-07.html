⭐
The star: "towing yarra valley"

Four leads for $42 — just $10.54 each, at an 8.9% CTR. The cheapest lead source in the account by a wide margin, and still running on a small share of budget.

📍
Pakenham has become a second stronghold

"towing pakenham" and "pakenham towing" together delivered 8 leads at ~$29 each, with CTRs of 23% and 30% — the highest engagement in the account. The Pakenham corridor is behaving exactly like the Yarra Valley did.

🎯
Suburb terms convert at extraordinary rates

Berwick (33% CTR), Pakenham (30%), Healesville (29%) — when someone searches their own suburb, they're ready to call. These are the terms to expand.

⚠️
The near-me terms are still the weak link

"tow truck near me" (Quality Score 4) and "tow trucks near me" (Quality Score 1) took $297 for 5.5 leads. "tow truck near me" has improved a lot since June — from $149 per lead down to $53.70 — but a Quality Score of 1 to 4 means Google is charging a penalty premium on every click. A tighter, more relevant landing page is still the fix.

06 · Action plan

Worth watching

📈
Average CPC climbed again — $11.57 → $14.86

Third month of increases, up 28% on June. Lead volume is more than covering it for now, but it's the single biggest threat to cost per lead. Driven by competition on the broad metro terms and by the low Quality Scores on the near-me keywords.

🚫
~$174 went to searches that will never convert

Zero-conversion clicks on competitor names (sheen towing bayswater, condello towing, western general towing), off-service queries (race car transporters, motorcycle transport, car transport interstate near me) and junk terms. That's ~8% of the month's spend, recoverable with negative keywords.

📊
Conversion value still isn't tracked

Leads are counted but not valued, so we still can't report revenue or ROAS. An average job value is all we need to unlock dollar-return reporting — this is the one thing holding the reporting back.

Recommended next steps

  • Add the negative keyword list — block competitor names, interstate/vehicle-transport queries and junk terms. Recovers roughly $174/month straight away.
  • Expand the suburb play — Pakenham, Berwick, Healesville and Lilydale are all converting at $13–$27. Build out the neighbouring towns on the same pattern.
  • Fix the near-me landing page — Quality Scores of 1 and 4 are taxing every click on the highest-volume terms. Better page relevance pulls CPC down account-wide.
  • Set a conversion value — so next month's report shows actual return, not just lead count.
  • Decide on the three paused campaigns — two P-Max and one search campaign are still sitting idle.
